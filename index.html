PRIVATE PERSONAL TOOL

Carter Calendar Executive

A private scheduling assistant used by its owner to coordinate school, work, training, meals, travel, recovery, and sleep in Google Calendar.

What it does

  • Reads the owner’s Google Calendar to identify commitments and conflicts.
  • Creates, updates, and removes calendar events only at the owner’s direction or under explicitly authorized scheduling rules.
  • Uses Calendar access solely to maintain the owner’s personal schedule.

Data practices

This is not a public service and does not accept accounts from other users. Google user data is not sold, advertised against, or shared with third parties.
