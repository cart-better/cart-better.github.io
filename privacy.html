Privacy Policy

Scope

Carter Calendar Executive is a private personal tool operated solely for its owner. It is not offered to the public and does not support third-party user accounts.

Google data accessed

The tool requests Google Calendar access. It may read calendar lists, calendars, event details, dates, times, descriptions, locations, recurrence information, and related event metadata. It may create, update, or delete events when authorized by the owner.

How data is used

Google Calendar data is used only to coordinate the owner’s schedule, identify conflicts, preserve sleep and meals, plan realistic transitions, and perform calendar changes requested or authorized by the owner.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and working scheduling data are stored locally in the owner-controlled Hermes Agent profile. Calendar data may be retained locally only as needed for scheduling state, verification, troubleshooting, and continuity. Access can be revoked through the owner’s Google Account permissions at any time.

Sharing and sale

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties. Data is disclosed only when required by law or when the owner explicitly directs a service interaction.

Deletion

The owner can revoke Google access through Google Account settings and delete locally stored credentials and scheduling records from the Hermes Agent profile.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions may be directed to the project owner using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
